Option Inputs & Interactive Greeks

Change the stock move, implied-volatility change, elapsed time, interest-rate move and contracts to calculate a local Taylor approximation. The underlying benchmark is European Black–Scholes; American CRR price remains separate.

Greek Values and Units

Delta = $ option change per $1 stock move; Gamma = delta change per $1 stock move; Vega per +1.00 volatility and +1 percentage point; Theta per calendar day (negative for time passage) and per year; Rho per +1.00 rate and +1 percentage point. All Greek values are per option share; multiply by 100 for one contract.
